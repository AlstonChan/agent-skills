## "The program does not start"

Work down this list:

1. **Is it already open?** [[APP_NAME]] runs once at a time. Look for its window on the taskbar
   and on any other desktop.
2. **Did you restart the computer since it was installed?** Restart it, then open
   [[APP_NAME]] again.
3. **Is there a message?** Look it up by its title in the message index (@sec-message-index).
4. **Still stuck?** Write down the exact words of any message and tell your administrator.
