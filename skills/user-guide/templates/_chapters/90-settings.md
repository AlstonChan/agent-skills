The settings decide how [[APP_NAME]] looks and behaves on this computer. Change them when the
defaults do not suit the way you work; nothing here alters your data.

## Open the settings window {#sec-settings-open}

1. Choose **Settings** in the menu bar.
2. Select the page you want from the list on the left.
3. Change the value, then press **Save**. Press **Cancel** to leave without changing anything.

![The settings window.](images/90-settings.png){#fig-settings width=90%}

## What each setting does

| Setting | What it does | Default |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Language** | The language of menus, messages and this guide's labels. | English |
| **Confirm before deleting** | Asks you to confirm each time you remove an item (@sec-[[AREA_ID]]). | On |
| **Show status bar** | Shows or hides the bar along the bottom of the main window. | On |

A change takes effect as soon as you press **Save**; you do not have to restart [[APP_NAME]].
